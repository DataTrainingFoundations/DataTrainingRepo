- If you were designing a full county database, what fact and dimension tables would you include?
- How would you find people dodging taxes this year vs the past 5 years?
- What is a surrogate key?
- What is an alternate key?
- Why choose a primary key over an alternate key?
- What joins do you know?
- Types of databases (OLAP vs OLTP)
- Primary vs Foreign keys; surrogate vs natural keys
- Difference between ETL and ELT pipelines
- Build a database schema (fact + dimension tables)
- Hackerrank completion scenario and debugging missing rows
- Why OLTP is used for web apps instead of OLAP
- Explain joins and visualize with Venn diagrams
- SQL vs NoSQL databases
- Why use SQL?
- Count students in a database course in 2024
- Find students who took multiple courses in same year
- Debug mismatched counts
- Top 5 products query
- DISTINCT vs GROUP BY
